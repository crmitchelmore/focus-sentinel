# The floor `scripts/glyph-distance.py` holds the status marks above (F-146, focus-w41).
#
# ONE NUMBER, and it is a measurement rather than a preference. `FocusStatus` has claimed since
# e01 that "silhouettes are deliberately unalike … even in monochrome at small sizes". Until e11b
# the evidence for that was P-132: a person looked at five SF Symbols in the real menu bar, once,
# and agreed. The symbols no longer ship. This file is what replaced the person.
#
# WHAT IS MEASURED. All ten images the menu bar can draw — five states, each with and without the
# attention mark composited into its top-trailing corner — rendered at 16 pt at 1× as coverage
# masks, box-downsampled by two and blurred twice with a 3×3 binomial kernel, compared pairwise
# as the mean absolute difference in 0–255 units. Blurred, because two marks that differ only in
# a detail a squint destroys are two marks a person glancing at a menu bar cannot tell apart.
#
# THE MEASURED SPREAD, on the family committed at focus-w41.3:
#
#     10.75   uncertain+mark  vs  onBreak+mark        the tightest pair, and the one that
#                                                     forced the design amendment
#     13.19   uncertain+mark  vs  unrelated+mark
#     15.08   relevant        vs  onBreak             the presence/absence pair the direction
#                                                     calls the strongest cue it has
#     …
#     61.85   relevant+mark   vs  unrelated           the widest
#
# THE FLOOR AND ITS MARGIN. 9.0 — the measured minimum less about 16 %. Wide enough that an
# antialiasing difference or a one-pixel nudge cannot redden the gate; tight enough that the pair
# the direction's own figure would have shipped (**1.43**, the 60°-gap `uncertain` against the
# plain ring of `onBreak`) is nowhere near it, and neither is the 4.46 the 120° amendment reached
# once the mark was composited.
#
# RAISING IT. Raise this the way `Config/test-floors.txt` is raised: in the same commit as the
# family that legitimately produced more, never to make a run green. LOWERING IT is a statement
# that two of the app's five states may look alike at the size they ship at, and needs a bead
# saying which two and why.
9.0
